Frequently asked questions

Is TradeX AI connected to a broker?

No. TradeX AI is a paper-trading simulation. It does not place real-money orders or connect to a brokerage account.

How do I reset my paper balance?

Sign in, open your trading dashboard and use “Reset paper account” to set a new virtual starting balance.

Why is a market feed unavailable?

Live data depends on the upstream market-data provider and its availability or credentials. A missing feed does not create a simulated live quote.

How do I report a login problem?

Choose “Account access” in the support form and describe the error. Do not send passwords or OTP codes.
